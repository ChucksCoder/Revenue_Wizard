Assumptions & notes
  • All results are per month. Tax and NI thresholds are the official 2026/27 rUK annual figures divided by 12, applied as a steady monthly rate — a simplified, non-cumulative view rather than exact PAYE month-by-month calculation.
  • "Employer NI saved" is measured against a no-sacrifice baseline: the employer NI they would have paid on the full gross pay, minus the employer NI on the reduced (post-sacrifice) pay. Employer pension contributions carry no NI.
  • Qualifying-earnings band per month: lower £520, upper £4,189 (contributions on the slice between). Employer NI secondary threshold £417/month, rate 15%.
  • Personal allowance taper (£1 lost per £2 of monthly-equivalent income above £8,333, fully gone above £10,428) is applied automatically.
  • Excludes student-loan repayments, Scottish tax, benefits-in-kind, apprenticeship levy, and the Employment Allowance (an employer-level relief, not per employee).
  • Salary sacrifice cannot take contractual pay below National Minimum/Living Wage — check this for lower-paid or part-time staff before offering it.
  • Planning illustration, not tax advice — confirm with a payroll or pensions adviser before acting.
